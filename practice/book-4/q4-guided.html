Question 4 · Comparing amounts

Linda and Serene had an equal number of balloons. After Linda used 16 balloons, Serene had 3 times as many balloons as Linda. How many balloons did Serene have?

Take your time

1Find the “after” relationship

Read the question. Which sentence tells you how their amounts compare after Linda used some balloons?

Hint: Read this closely: “Serene had 3 times as many balloons as Linda.” Who has the bigger amount?

Afterwards: Serene = 3 equal parts; Linda = 1 part
You can now find one unit.
Extra balloons = 3 units − 1 unit = 2 units
16 balloons = 2 units
1 unit = 16 ÷ 2 = 8
Serene has 3 units. What is 3 × 8?
